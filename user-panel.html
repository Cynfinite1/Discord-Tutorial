Subsection 4.3

Your User Panel

The user panel sits at the bottom of the channel list. It shows your picture and name and three buttons that control your sound and settings.

The three buttons

ButtonWhat it does
MicrophoneClick it to mute or unmute your microphone. A red line through the icon means you are muted.
HeadphonesClick it to deafen or undeafen yourself. A red line through the icon means you hear no sound.
GearOpens User Settings, where you change your profile, language, and text size.

Muting compared with deafening

Muting turns off only your microphone, so you can still hear your group. Deafening turns off all sound in Discord and also mutes your microphone. Before a meeting, check that neither button has a red line through it.

NoteClick your picture or name in the user panel to see your profile card and change your status, such as Online or Do Not Disturb.
