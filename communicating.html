Section 5

Communicating with Your Group

This section teaches you how to talk with your group and how to make sure you never miss a meeting change or deadline.

You need: Discord open to a text channel in your group's server.

  • 5.1 Sending Messages and Direct Messages

    Type, edit, reply, react, message privately, and use threads.

  • 5.2 Using Mentions

    Notify one teammate, a role, or the whole server.

  • 5.3 Finding Messages You Missed

    Unread indicators, the Inbox, pinned messages, and search.

  • 5.4 Sharing and Finding Files

    Upload documents and find them again later.
