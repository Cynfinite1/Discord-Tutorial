Getting Started: Creating Your Account

Nothing else in this tutorial works until you have an account, so this section comes first. You will install the desktop app, create your account, and set up a profile your teammates recognize.

You need: your computer and an email address you can check.

  • 2.1 Downloading the Desktop App

    Install Discord on a Windows PC.

  • 2.2 Signing Up and Verifying Your Email

    Register, choose your names, and confirm your email address.

  • 2.3 Setting Up Your Profile and Comfort Settings

    Add an avatar and pronouns, change the language, and make text larger.
