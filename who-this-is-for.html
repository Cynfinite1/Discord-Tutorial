Subsection 1.2

Who This Tutorial Is For

This tutorial is for students who have never used Discord and need it for a class, group project, or student organization.

Who will get the most from this tutorial

You will get the most from this tutorial if you:

  • Already use email, Canvas, any messaging app such as WhatsApp, and Zoom
  • Have a group that asked you to join their Discord server
  • Want to keep up with messages without spending hours learning a new app

What you need before you start

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC
  • An internet connection
  • An email address you can check right away, such as your Knights email
  • A microphone and speakers or headphones for voice and video calls

CautionLibrary and work computers often block new programs from being installed. Use your own computer for this tutorial so you can install the desktop app.

What you do not need to know

You do not need to know any Discord words before you begin. Every new word appears in blue text the first time it is used on a page. Point to the blue word with your mouse to read its definition. All new words are also collected in the Glossary.
