Subsection 5.1

Sending Messages and Direct Messages

Most group work in Discord happens in typed messages. This page shows you how to send, edit, and answer messages and how to talk privately.

Send a message

  1. Click a text channel in the channel list.
  2. Click the message box at the bottom of the chat area. It says Message # followed by the channel name.
  3. Type your message.
  4. Press Enter to send it.

Result: Your message appears at the bottom of the chat area for everyone in the channel.

NoteTo start a new line without sending, press Shift + Enter.

Edit a message you sent

  1. Hold your pointer over your message.
  2. Click the pencil icon that appears on the right.
  3. Change the text and press Enter.

Result: The message updates and shows (edited) next to it.

Reply to a message

A reply shows everyone which message you are answering, which keeps busy channels clear.

  1. Hold your pointer over the message you want to answer.
  2. Click the Reply arrow on the right.
  3. Type your answer and press Enter.

React to a message

A reaction is a quick way to say "I saw this" without adding another message.

  1. Hold your pointer over the message.
  2. Click the smiley face icon (Add Reaction).
  3. Click an emoji, such as a thumbs up.

Send a direct message

A direct message (DM) is a private conversation outside the server channels.

  1. Click a teammate's name in the member list.
  2. Type your message in the Message @ box on their profile card.
  3. Press Enter.

Result: A private conversation opens. To find it later, click the Discord logo at the top of the server list.

Start a thread

A thread keeps a long side discussion about one message out of the main channel.

  1. Hold your pointer over the message you want to discuss.
  2. Click the three dots (More) and then click Create Thread.
  3. Type a short name for the thread.
  4. Type your first message and press Enter.
