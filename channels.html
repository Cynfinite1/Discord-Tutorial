Subsection 4.2

Text Channels and Voice Channels

Every server has two main kinds of channels. You can tell them apart by the icon in front of each channel name.

Text channels

A text channel has a pound sign (#) in front of its name, such as #announcements. Text channels are for typed messages, links, and files. Click a text channel to read it.

Voice channels

A voice channel has a speaker icon in front of its name, such as Meeting Room. Voice channels are for live talking, video, and screen sharing.

CautionClicking a voice channel connects you to it immediately, and the other people in it may hear your microphone. Click a voice channel only when you are ready to talk.

Bold and gray channel names

A channel name in bold white has messages you have not read. A gray channel name means you are caught up. Subsection 5.3 explains more ways to catch up.
