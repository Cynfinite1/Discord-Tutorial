10. Glossary

This glossary defines every new word in this tutorial in alphabetical order. The same definitions appear when you point to a blue word on any page.

More words will be added as the remaining sections are written.

Attachment
A file, such as a document, slide deck, or image, that you add to a message.
Avatar
Your profile picture. It appears next to every message you send.
CAPTCHA
A short puzzle, such as a checkbox or picture question, that proves you are a person and not a program.
Category
A heading in the channel list that groups related channels, such as TEXT CHANNELS or VOICE CHANNELS.
Channel
A separate area inside a server for one topic or activity. A server can have many channels.
Channel list
The column next to the server list that shows every channel in the server you selected.
Chat area
The large center area of Discord that shows the messages in the channel you selected.
Deafen
To turn off all sound coming into Discord. Deafening also mutes your microphone.
Desktop app
The version of Discord you download and install on a Windows PC.
Direct message
A private conversation between you and one person or a small group, outside of any server. Often shortened to DM.
Display name
The name other people see next to your messages. It does not have to be unique.
Inbox
The tray icon at the top right of Discord that collects your recent mentions and unread messages in one list.
Invite link
A web address, usually starting with discord.gg/, that lets you join a specific server.
Member list
The column on the right side of a server that shows who belongs to the server and who is online.
Mention
Typing the @ symbol followed by a name so that person receives a notification. Also called a ping.
Mention badge
A red circle with a number that shows how many times someone mentioned you.
Moderator
A server member who manages channels, roles, and members, and keeps the server organized.
Mute
To turn off your microphone so no one can hear you. You can still hear others.
Pinned message
An important message that a group saves to a channel's pinned list so it is easy to find later.
Reaction
A small emoji added under a message. Groups often use reactions to say "I saw this" or to vote.
Reply
A message that is attached to an earlier message so everyone can see what you are answering.
Role
A label a server gives to a group of members, such as @Presenters. Roles can control who sees certain channels.
Server
A private space on Discord for one group, class, or club. Each server has its own channels and members.
Server list
The column of round icons on the far left of Discord. Each icon is a server you belong to.
Server nickname
A name you use in only one server. It replaces your display name in that server and nowhere else.
Text channel
A channel marked with a pound sign (#) where people type messages and share files.
Thread
A side conversation attached to one message in a channel, so a long discussion does not crowd the main channel.
Unread indicator
A white dot or bold channel name that shows there are new messages you have not read.
User panel
The small bar at the bottom left of Discord with your name, your microphone button, your headphone button, and the gear icon.
User Settings
The menu where you change your account, profile, language, and text size. Open it with the gear icon at the bottom left.
Username
Your unique account name. No two Discord accounts can have the same username.
Voice channel
A channel marked with a speaker icon where people talk live with their voice, camera, or screen.
