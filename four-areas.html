4.1 The Four Main Areas of the Screen

The Discord window is divided into four main areas. Read them from left to right.

1. Server list

The server list is the narrow column of round icons on the far left. Each icon is a server you belong to. The Discord logo at the top opens your private messages, which Subsection 5.1 explains.

2. Channel list

The channel list shows every channel in the server you selected. The server name appears at the top. Channels are grouped under headings called categories.

3. Chat area

The chat area in the center shows the messages in the channel you selected. The newest messages are at the bottom, and the box for typing your own message is below them.

4. Member list

The member list on the right shows who belongs to the server. Members who are online appear at the top.

NoteIf you do not see the member list, click the icon of two people at the top-right of the window to show or hide it.
