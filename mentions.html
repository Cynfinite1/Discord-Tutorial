Subsection 5.2

Using Mentions

A mention, also called a ping, sends a notification to the people you name. Use mentions when someone needs to see a message soon.

Mention a teammate

  1. In the message box, type @.
  2. Type the first few letters of your teammate's name.
  3. Click their name in the list that appears.
  4. Finish your message and press Enter.

Result: Your teammate receives a notification, and the message is highlighted for them.

NoteWhen someone mentions you, the message has a yellow highlight and a yellow bar on its left edge, so you can spot it while scrolling.

Mention a group of people

Three special mentions reach more than one person:

MentionWho is notifiedWhen to use it
@everyoneEvery member of the serverRarely, for changes that affect the whole group, such as a new meeting time
@hereOnly members who are online right nowFor something happening now, such as "the call is starting"
@ and a role nameOnly members with that role, such as @PresentersFor tasks that involve part of the group

CautionOverusing @everyone and @here annoys teammates and can lead them to turn off notifications. Many servers allow only a moderator to use them.
