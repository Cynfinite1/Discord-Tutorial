1. Introduction: What Discord Is and Why Your Group Uses It

Discord is a free app that keeps a group's messages, files, and meetings in one place. This page explains how Discord is organized and why your group chose it.

What Discord does

Discord combines three tools that groups usually keep separate:

  • Typed chat for questions, updates, and links
  • Voice and video calls for meetings
  • File sharing for drafts, slides, and images

Each group gets its own server. Inside the server, conversations are split into channels, and each channel has one topic, such as announcements or file drafts. Because topics stay separate, you can go straight to the information you need instead of scrolling through one long chat.

Why classes and clubs choose Discord

Many UCF project groups and student organizations use Discord for four reasons:

  • It is free: Every feature in this tutorial works without a paid plan.
  • It is organized by topic: Announcements, files, and casual chat each have their own channel.
  • Meetings need no link: A meeting room stays open, so members join with one click instead of waiting for a new meeting link.
  • It works on every device: The desktop app, the browser version, and the phone app all show the same messages.

NoteDiscord is also popular for gaming, but you do not need any gaming experience. A school server only contains the channels your group creates.
