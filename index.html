Section 1

Introduction

Your group says everything is on Discord, but you are not sure where to look. This tutorial will teach you how to use the Discord desktop app for a class or group project, from creating your account to joining your group's meetings.

Discord is a free app, made by Discord Inc., for typed chat, voice calls, video calls, and file sharing. You can use Discord on a computer, in a web browser, or on a phone. This tutorial covers only the desktop app for Windows, so every step and every picture matches what you see on your computer screen.

What you will be able to do

  • Create an account and set up a profile your teammates recognize
  • Join your group's server and find the right channels
  • Send messages, reply, and share files
  • Find deadlines and meeting changes you missed
  • Join voice meetings and share your screen

Before you begin

Skill levelYou only need to be comfortable with email, a web browser and messaging apps.
You needA Windows 10 or 11 PC, an internet connection, and an email address you can access
Voice/Video CallsA microphone and speakers or headphones. A webcam is optional.
